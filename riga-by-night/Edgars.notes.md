# Background

Edgars Holm is the grand-son of the children of famous publisher, translator, and author (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korfiz_Holm). His father was born and created in Germany, and had a small bookshop in Munich. Growing up in that bookshop, filled with Latvian books about lost German crusaders treasures and other histories from the port of Riga lead the young Edgars to get interested in history since an early age. After his graduation from the university of Munich, he decided to dedicate himself to archeology in Riga inspired by the Singing Revolution movement.

His embrace came after multiple archeology conferences he attended as part of his Master and PhD on the topic of ancient relics that passed through Riga over the years, from  viking tablets with rites and spells, to baltic pagan scrolls with encantations, to teutonic/crusader religious texts with encrypted ritual (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livonian_Crusade).

After his PhD he stayed in Riga and works as a curator in the National Library of Latvia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Library_of_Latvia), cataloguing and finding relics for them (and replacing with forgeries the ones that are valuable enough). After his PhD he stayed in Riga, working as a curator in the National Library of Latvia in Riga, cataloguing and finding relics for them (and replacing with forgeries the ones that are valuable enough). He often travels to coordinate new acquisitions, meet potential new sellers and their discoveries, or meet his sire (who lives in Prague), or "organise" the museums storage (https://www.vni.lv/projekti/muzeju-kratuve).

Sire is the primogen of Tremere in Prague, that ran away from Vienna and made a new base for himself in Prague. He was embraced because of his access to such museum relics. Edgar have some serious contacts in Camarilla, but not the Riga/local one. In the local Camarilla, he mostly operates with his own authority. But if you really need it your sire can extend his influence.

In Riga, he found texts for house Carna about druidic rituals and got connected to them. Edgar shares Carna belief on the freedom to study and practice magic away from the shackles of his sire and clan. However, he keeps that connection hidden from his Camarilla associates and his sire. His beliefs are mostly shown when motivating the people in his occultism research group to practice by themselves - he often mentors them on sharing their successes and failures with the group in multiple occasions.

Edgar before the embrace was a passionate historian, fascinated by tales of medieval crusades passing by Latvia, a land where his grandfather came from but he never visited himself - for some reason, his father hated to talk about his childhood there, witch only peaked the curiosity of young Edgar. His facination only grew, turning to obsession, after deciding to move to Riga, where he could spot (imaginary) historic clues around the city. Eventually such obsession lead him to his PhD topic: ancient relics that passed through Riga over the years. Such a topic led him to create a research group with some students: a archeologist (Paul), a geologist (Rick), and a urban-geography (Lana). The group took turns presenting their recent findings on relics in Riga, open to the public for a small fee to join. In one of the early sessions, such a group was visited by a Dominik Marek, who presented himself as a investor that was willing to fund the group sessions in exchange of early reports about their findings.Dominik couldn't phisically join all sessions, so for a time Edgars travelled to Prague by train in the weekends to deliver him some new material (for some reason, Dominik didn't trust electronic nor analogic mails, claiming all was being tracked). Such travels happened for the entirety of Edgar's PhD and led to a relationship to be formed, as he and Dominik always chatted about the history of Riga throuout the centuries and the many groups that came and went or sought refugee there for a time. Dominik often shared stories about ancient wizards who could talk to the ones that came before, or even see the world through their memories, which always fascinated the young Edgar (imagine, be able to see history with your own eys as if you're there!). In one occasion, Dominik told Erik about the Ialdabaoth Codex, a grimoire that was allegedly held by someone in the University of Riga. Edgar, by his own initiative (he thought), discovered the book was in the house of the headmaster of the history department, broken in his house, and stole it. He presented Dominik with it, who, recognising the value of such associate, revealed his true nature to Edgar and embraced him. Such move came at the same time that Edgar's father was on his deathbed, so a difficult choice between his obsession and his ties to humanity was made. For a month, they tried to decode the grimoire together, and Edgar was thaught about blood magic in the process, which helped him to not give a second thought about his father. Without much (apparent) sucesss, Edgar was instructed to go back to Riga to search for similar artifacts, with the promisse of more teachings if he succeeded. For that purpose, he used his new persuading talents for getting a job at the museum (Dominik may have granted him some historical apparatus for him to impress his new boss).Edgar understand his sire game and, while he see the advantage on keeping it, hide his disdain for such Victorian knowledge gate-keeping tactic. As a researcher from a field that papers are freely distributed, he can't understand why one shouldn't have access to whatever material they desire. Such sharing practice would benefit the Tremere as a whole, he thinks, as bringuing more perspectives to magic problems may only help solving them. Therefore, he opened up the research group to amateurs, that both would learn about artifacts (and maybe some magic rituals here and there) and serve as test subjects (for whatever new ritual). Bringuing amateurs and occultism subjects to the table didn't please the other founders of the group, who eventually resented Edgar and moved out, after many heated arguments.The "research" group today is named Eyes-Wide-Open, advertising itself as a place where the hidden reality is uncovered and explained. Most people come for one session and them disappear again (easy preys for a eloquent vampire), but some stay for longer. They always organise their sessions in different places (e.g. a bookstore in the shopping center after it closes, an old mansion in the outskirts, a large crypt in the cemitery). Bethany is one old-widow who is always there, bringuing refreshments to all as her cats don't make up for good company and she hopes to one day be able to talk to her late husband who died some years ago. Sometimes her nephew Igor comes along (because it is Bethany turn to babysit for him), who dabbles in technology more than he should and therefore takes care of storing and protecting the material that is shared and the Telegram groups that advertise where the next session will be. As the group grew, it got the attention of a blogger, a young american lady (Sarah) who thinks she can become a real journalist if she bring proof that weird things happen in eastern europe to some America's publication. She managed to visit some sessions, by stealing cellphones from others and them discovering where the next meeting would be, and even wrote a piece about it in her blog - that thankfully didn't attract much attention due to the lack of evidence that such a session really occurred. She heard about Edgar but never meat him, so discovering more about his whereabouts and motivations is part of her plan.
During one group session in an old church, Edgar got a feeling something more could be found and stayed a while longer. His guess paid out as he found a tunnel access in the basement to an abandoned bunker. He found the old priest (Jeremiah) corpse there and some bookcases with old satanic books. Through the priest diary, Edgar found out he was a communist that truly believed the Berlin-wall-fall would start WW3, and isolated himself in the bunker for a few days for safety. The door got stuck (some landslide affected the mechanism and it could only open from the outside) and there is where he died, blaming his fate on his studies of Satan. The ghost of Jeremiah still haunts the bunker, where he sometimes preach to Edgar, sometimes play chess, and sometimes have deep conversations about blood magic, faith, or the state of the decadent state of world where the state doesn't provide enough to a farmers' family.
