
The night is lightless in this part of the city, due to the remaining clouds that were brought with the last days' heavy rains and the broken streetlamps that were brought by government apathy. A lonely light shines upon the bus stop, and the marker shows the next ride coming in five minutes. A single woman, under a hoodie, walks towards the light bus stop, tightening in her grip a heavy paper bag.

Her mind is carried away by the vision of the light and the thoughts of a warm meal and she fails to notice she is not completely alone. Three figures appear from behind the stop, three boys with ragtag clothing, menacing eyes, and broken improvised pipes being waved as weapons. One of them notices the woman, smiles, and waves to the others making jokes about such a break from their boredom, before greeting her:

"Good evening ma'am, shall we carry such a heavy bag for you" 

"Ow no it's not a problem at all, don't bother yourself kiddo"
